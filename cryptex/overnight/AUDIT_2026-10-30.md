**Scope: independently verified paper trading only, not actual money or investment advice.** No real exchange orders, exchange keys, leverage, deposits or withdrawals are enabled.

- The shadow engine suffered a saved-state schema migration error at 22:16; the 22:21 automated run verified the non-destructive migration repaired it. Existing paper cash and positions were preserved.
- A normal GitHub cron event remains **unverified** despite active configuration and staggered 5-minute schedule. Independent workflow_dispatch follow-ups, including 22:11, 22:16 and 22:21, have genuinely run and saved paper journal updates without human intervention.
- The bounded overnight fallback runs from 21:00 Dublin time until it records the first fresh after-08:00 morning balance (hard 08:15 cutoff), or reaches its 132-cycle cap. It is **not permanent 24/7 hosting**. It does not automatically start the *next night's* backup if native cron remains absent.

## Improvements implemented in this audit

1. **Cost and error attribution:** From newly completed paper SELL orders onward, record entry/exit market candle mid-prices, gross directional P&L, total estimated fee/slippage drag, and final net P&L. Earlier trades without these fields are explicitly marked unknown, not reconstructed using made-up observations.
2. **Break-even analysis:** The research report calculates about **0.8032%** necessary market appreciation just to overcome the assumed 0.15% fee and 0.25% slippage on both sides of a buy-and-sell round trip. High turnover will frequently lose money if this hurdle is not exceeded.
3. **Shadow portfolio risk controls:** New 5% daily loss pause and 15% peak drawdown stop on the eleven experimental accounts. The original portfolio retains its limits. Existing shadow states migrate without resetting paper funds or past trades.
4. **Prospective comparisons:** Three completely separate $10 fictitious BTC, ETH and SOL buy-and-hold reference positions are frozen at their first real timestamp, use the same hypothetical execution costs, and never contribute to the main $210 trading balance. They are benchmarks, not extra traded capital.
5. **Morning correctness:** The backup now covers the first verified after-08:00 local morning snapshot before quitting, rather than stopping just before the morning balance becomes observable.
6. **GitHub consistency:** The trading journal save step retries normal Git rebasing after concurrent code edits without force-pushing or silently overwriting newer market data. New code pushes do not seed parallel backup chains.
7. **Money-dashboard clarity:** The dashboard now presents cost break-even, verified gross-vs-net win count, estimated execution drag, and BTC benchmark beside its balance and 21-agent leaderboard.

## Actual scientific findings

This initial study **has no demonstrated financial trading edge**. At 22:11 UTC, all 14 closed round trips had lost after costs. Early simulated realised P&L and paper mark-to-market are negative. That is data, not a failure to display trades.

External research strengthens the reason for measuring costs and avoiding premature claims:

- GitHub documents that scheduled workflows may be delayed or dropped under load: https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/workflows-and-actions/events-that-trigger-workflows
- A 2026 cryptocurrency market-microstructure study found that apparently useful five-minute forecasts can be wiped out by trading friction: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/blockchain/articles/10.3389/fbloc.2026.1811716/full
- Multiple-signal backtesting has high overfitting risk, particularly where variants share information: https://www.nber.org/papers/w21329
- Model-selection bias and non-normal returns require much stronger evidence than selecting a winning bot after a few rounds: https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2014.40.5.094

## Remaining work, in priority order

1. **Genuine 24/7 host:** GitHub Actions is not a permanent server. The auto-trigger backup is intentionally bounded and the native cron has no confirmed schedule-event execution. A protected dedicated persistent worker with storage, logs, uptime monitoring and failure recovery is preferable to endlessly sleeping GitHub Actions runners. Do not spend money or modify existing DigitalOcean servers without approval.
2. **Forward performance and ablations:** Keep model code/thresholds versioned while collecting fresh, fee-adjusted samples. Test the effect of each mathematical hypothesis, factor family and trading exit against BTC/ETH/SOL benchmarks. Require multiple market regimes and out-of-sample checks; correct for testing many variants.
3. **Better execution data:** Confirm real bid/ask spread, order-book depth, latency, symbol precision, taker/maker fees, liquidity and possible partial fills before any claim of real-world performance.
4. **News research sophistication:** A small locally runnable, permissively licensed article classifier could be tested against the current keyword baseline, using public/legal article access, timestamp provenance and held-out labels. Public headlines are not reliable trading instructions.
5. **Do not mistake complexity for predictive power:** The 640 correlated subagents are diagnostic, not independent trained market experts. The five GitHub frameworks supply architecture patterns but are not wholesale integrations.
6. **Validate the actual morning checkpoint on 31 October 2026:** Confirm that the backup executed and the first fresh after-08:00 balance was frozen. If no run took place, flag missing data rather than fabricating a balance.
