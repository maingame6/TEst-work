QUANTITATIVE RESEARCH / 03

Inside the algorithms

Inspectable rules, cost assumptions and experimental mathematical models.

Research tiers

Empirically investigated: risk-scaled momentum, trend, reversal, relative-strength rotations. Mechanism-based but cost-sensitive: grid, breakouts, volume spikes. Experimental analogies: Navier–Stokes-inspired flow, fractal entropy and cross-coin shockwaves. A known mathematical result is not proof of trading edge.

"Spy" uses volume proxies, not copied wallets. The live quote mode does not include verified wallet transactions, historical order books, or on-chain contract security checks. Real execution, short-selling and on-chain gas are not implemented.

Select a research module

Inspired by mathematical AI breakthroughs

Navier–Stokes: fluid velocity, diffusion and turbulence inspire a *discrete flow heuristic* in Bot 08—not a solution of the PDE and not a predictive theorem. OpenAI announced an AI-generated proof in September 2026; Clay said the problem apparently had been settled, with formal review ongoing.

FunSearch / cap-set / bin packing: AI discovers candidate algorithms and an external evaluator tests them. This suggests systematic searches for trading rules using strict holdout evaluation; it does not supply a financial edge.

2026 Erdős problems / Lean verification: AI assisted in settling multiple hard combinatorial and number-theoretic problems. The transferable idea is independently checking calculations and risk invariants, not importing theorems as price predictions.

AlphaTensor / AlphaEvolve: optimizing matrix procedures and discovering verified algorithms suggests automated model comparison, robust constraints and risk-efficient feature calculations. It does not mean a strategy wins money.

Fractal patterns, entropy and lead/lag networks: these are testable mathematical hypotheses, explicitly experimental here.

Simulator disclosure

Simulation candles and volumes are generated with seeded pseudorandom market factors, volatility regimes and correlated coin shocks. They are not historical data or a faithful market microstructure model. Signals are generated at the close of one observation and paper fills occur at the *next* observation. Fractional spot positions only. Trades use configurable taker fees, spread and slippage; no interest, funding or exchange minimums. Market impact and token gas are only approximated. Public live prices can be delayed, rate-limited or unavailable. No real-money orders are possible.
